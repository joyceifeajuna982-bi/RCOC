Daily Fire Altar

Today's Prophetic Prayer Points

Engage these scriptures and bullet points with holy aggression and faith.

1. Prayer for Total Restoration of Lost Years

Scripture: Joel 2:25 - "And I will restore to you the years that the locust hath eaten..."

Prayer Point: Father, by your mighty hand, I command a supernatural restoration of every wasted year, wasted opportunity, and stolen blessings in Jesus' name!

2. Divine Health & Immunity

Scripture: Exodus 23:25 - "And ye shall serve the Lord your God... and I will take sickness away from the midst of thee."

Prayer Point: Every planted seed of infirmity in my body is flushed out by the blood of Jesus. I walk in divine health today!

3. Gates of Financial Open Doors

Scripture: Isaiah 45:1 - "Thus saith the Lord to his anointed, to Cyrus... to open before him the two leaved gates..."

Prayer Point: O Lord, lift up ancient gates and let economic favor, abundance, and uncommon financial breakthrough locate me now.
